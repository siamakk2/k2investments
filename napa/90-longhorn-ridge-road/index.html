Ninety Longhorn Ridge sits inside a private, gated equestrian community in the eastern foothills of Napa Valley — eleven parcels, a private paved road, and miles of riding and hiking trails. From the wraparound porch the view opens across rolling hills and vineyards to Lake Berryessa.

The house was built for gathering: an open plan running from a chef's kitchen with commercial appliances through the great room and family room, each opening to the outdoors. Outside there is a pool, hot tub, bocce court, fire pit, and a full outdoor kitchen with BBQ, sink and beer taps alongside covered dining.

The detached guest house is not a converted garage. It is a fully finished modern residence with its own kitchen, living area, vaulted ceilings with skylights, polished concrete floors and a built-in bunk room — suited to extended family, long-staying guests, staff or a private office.

Questions

What buyers ask.

What is Longhorn Ridge?

A private, gated equestrian community in the eastern foothills of Napa Valley — only eleven parcels across roughly 800 acres, with its own paved private road and miles of riding and hiking trails.

How far is it from downtown Napa?

About twenty minutes. From downtown Napa, take Trancas Street onto Monticello Road (Highway 121) roughly eight miles east; Longhorn Ridge Road is the gated private road on the right.

Is there a guest house?

Yes — a fully finished detached accessory dwelling with its own kitchen, living area, vaulted ceilings and a built-in bunk room.

What are the HOA dues?

$420 per month to the Longhorn Ridge Owners Association, which maintains the private paved road and gated entry.
