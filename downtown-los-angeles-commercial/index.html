Know your submarket

The three Downtown corridors where K2 is most active.

Downtown Los Angeles is not one market — it is a collection of distinct commercial submarkets, each with its own tenant base, rental dynamics and property character. Understanding which submarket fits your business or investment thesis is the first step to finding the right space.

Fashion District

Roughly bounded by 9th Street to Olympic Boulevard between Maple Avenue and Los Angeles Street. The largest wholesale commercial district in the U.S., home to apparel showrooms, retail storefronts, creative offices and a rapidly growing hospitality corridor. High foot traffic, strong tenant demand, and corner properties at a premium.

Current listing: 1048 S Los Angeles Street →

Flower District

Centered around E 8th Street between Wall Street and San Julian Street. One of Downtown LA's most commercially active and well-connected corridors — walkable to the Fashion District and close to major transit.

K2's office is at 530 E 8th Street · 6,700 SF penthouse loft for lease →

Garment District · Santee

The Santee Street corridor from Olympic to 9th Street runs through the heart of the district and includes some of DTLA's highest-density retail streets. The area supports light manufacturing, wholesale showrooms, fashion design studios and creative commercial tenants.

What is the difference between the Fashion, Flower and Garment Districts?

The Fashion District is the largest wholesale commercial district in the U.S. — apparel showrooms, retail storefronts and creative offices with high foot traffic. The Flower District, centred on E 8th Street, is one of DTLA's most connected corridors and close to major transit. The Garment District along Santee Street supports light manufacturing, wholesale showrooms and creative studios. Each has its own tenant base, rental dynamics and property character.
