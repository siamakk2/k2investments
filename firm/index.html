Our story

Not the largest firm by design. Boutique by conviction.

For over three decades, K2 Investment Inc. has served as a dedicated partner in our clients' most significant real estate decisions. The story began in 1994 in the dynamic heart of Downtown Los Angeles — in the Fashion, Flower and Garment Districts — where we forged our reputation on a simple, enduring principle: treat every client's asset as if it were our own.

While our expertise is deeply rooted in the Los Angeles market, our reach extends across California. We are not the largest firm by design — our boutique structure is our greatest competitive advantage. It allows us to provide customised, white-glove service that larger brokerages structurally cannot match.

Who we serve

Four distinct buyer profiles.

1031 Exchange Buyers

Investors with 45 days to identify and 180 days to close. K2 maintains an inventory of stabilized, income-producing DTLA assets that qualify as like-kind replacement property under IRC §1031.

Key advantage — Month-to-month leases open repositioning upside immediately after close.

Business Owner-Occupants

Occupy space for your own operations while building equity. SBA 504 financing available with as little as 10% down.

Key advantage — Own your DTLA address instead of paying rent — while you tenant-pay down the building.

Foreign & International Buyers

No California residency or foreign-national ownership restriction. USD-income assets, globally recognized market. K2 provides referrals to bilingual counsel and FIRPTA-experienced CPAs.

Key advantage — Dollar-denominated income in one of the world's most recognized real estate markets.

Private Investors & Family Offices

High-net-worth investors and family offices seeking documented, income-producing DTLA commercial assets with value-add potential.

Key advantage — Documented rent rolls and a disciplined, data-driven underwriting approach.
